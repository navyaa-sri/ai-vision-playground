2 Filter (3×3 kernel)

positive weight negative weight zero

For every output pixel, the kernel sits on top of the matching input pixel and its 8 neighbours. Each pixel is multiplied by the weight above it, and the 9 products are added together. Pixels beyond the edge count as 0.
